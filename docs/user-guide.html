Getting started

The big picture — what each feature is for, and the order to use them.

The CRM is built around a single repeatable flow. Once you understand the order, every other screen is just a step in the chain.

Step 1 · Audience

Define your ICP

Who are you actually trying to reach? Create an Ideal Customer Profile.

Step 2 · Catalogue

Add Products & Offers

What you sell, and how you bundle it for a specific ICP.

Step 3 · Leads

Import Contacts

Upload your list. Each contact is tagged with the right ICP.

Step 4 · Launch

Run a Campaign

The system handles outreach, replies, and tracking automatically.

Watch it work

Once a campaign is live, the Dashboard shows live metrics and the Journey page shows every lead’s timeline. You don’t need to do anything manual after launch.

2

ICP — Ideal Customer Profile

Define who you’re selling to. Every campaign needs at least one ICP.

Open it from the sidebar: Others › ICP.

1

Click “New ICP”

Top-right corner of the ICP page. A modal opens.

2

Name and describe the audience

Give it a clear, short name (e.g. “Mid-sized US parishes”) and a description explaining who fits.

3

Save

The ICP shows up in the table immediately and is ready to attach to an Offer.

Think of it this way

An ICP is a label for an audience. You can have many ICPs and reuse them across multiple Offers.

3

Products

Your catalogue — what you actually sell.

Open it from the sidebar: Others › Products.

1

Click “New Product”

Top-right of the Products page.

2

Fill in the product details

Enter the product name, choose a pricing type, and set the price.

3

Save

The product joins your catalogue and is selectable from any Offer.

Tip

Keep product names short and consistent — they appear in lots of other screens (Offers, Campaigns, Journey detail).

4

Offers

An Offer ties one or more Products to an ICP — it’s the “pitch” a campaign uses.

Open it from the sidebar: Others › Offers.

1

Click “New Offer”

Top-right of the Offers page.

2

Choose the ICP

Pick the audience this offer is aimed at. You can only pick from ICPs you’ve already created.

3

Attach one or more Products

Bundle whatever combination makes sense for that ICP.

4

Save

The Offer is now available when creating a Campaign.

Natural order

You can’t launch a campaign without an Offer. The order is always: ICP → Product → Offer → Campaign.

5

Contacts

The leads you’ll be reaching out to — imported once, used by every campaign.

Open it from the sidebar: Others › Contacts.

1

Add or import contacts

Use the upload or new-contact button to bring leads into the system.

2

Tag each contact with the right ICP

Campaigns target by ICP — an untagged contact will never be reached.

3

Spot-check before you launch

Scan the table: name, email, institution, ICP. Catching a typo here saves a wasted outreach later.

Heads up

Bad data in = bad outreach out. A bad email address means a bounce, and a wrong ICP means the wrong pitch. Always spot-check before launching a campaign.

6

Campaigns

Where everything comes together. A campaign automates outreach to your contacts.

Open it from the sidebar: Others › Campaigns.

1

Click “New campaign”

Top-right of the Campaigns page.

2

Configure the campaign

Give it a name, pick the Offer, set template / tone / send limits, and add any specific instructions you want the outreach agent to follow.

3

Save as Draft

New campaigns always start as Draft. Nothing is sent. This is your final review window.

4

Set status to “Active” to go live

On the campaign row, change the status dropdown from Draft to Active and confirm the prompt. That queues the campaign.

5

Wait ~10 minutes — it goes live automatically

Within roughly ten minutes, the outreach agent picks the campaign up and sends the first email. The status flips from Active to Running automatically as soon as that happens.

6

Stop a campaign any time

Change status to Stopped to halt all further sends for that campaign immediately.

Status lifecycle

DraftInactive — nothing sent
ActiveQueued — pickup in ~10 min
RunningAuto-set after first send
StoppedHalted manually
How campaign status works
  • Setting status to Active queues the campaign — it will auto-run within ~10 minutes.
  • Status flips to Running automatically once the first email outreach is dropped.
  • Use the status filter at the top of the table to narrow down to just Running or Active campaigns when you have many in flight.
Deleting a campaign is permanent

Deleting also removes every journey under that campaign and every outreach / event log attached to those journeys. The system will ask you to confirm with the word DELETE — that pause is intentional.

7

Journey

The story of every individual lead — what was sent, when, and how they replied.

Open it from the sidebar: Others › Journey.

1

Use the filters at the top

Narrow the list by campaign, stage, institution type, or date range. The search box matches parish, diocese, or email.

2

Read the funnel stages

Every lead is in one of these stages — moving left to right as they engage.

OutreachedFirst email sent
EngagedOpened / replied
MQLMarketing qualified
SALSales accepted
SQLSales qualified
Closed-WonDeal closed
3

Click a lead to see their full timeline

Opens a detail view showing every email sent, every reply, and every status change for that lead.

Journeys are read-only

The outreach agent writes to journeys automatically. You don’t edit a journey by hand — to change anything about how a lead is being reached, you edit the campaign or the contact.

8

Meetings

Calendar slots that leads have booked through the outreach flow.

Open it from the sidebar: Others › Meetings.

1

Browse upcoming bookings

See every meeting that’s been scheduled, with the lead’s name, time, and which campaign drove the booking.

2

Prep using the lead’s journey

Click any meeting to jump to that lead’s timeline. Use it to prep talking points based on what they’ve already engaged with.

Bookings are automatic

Calendly slots flow into this page on their own — no manual entry needed. If a meeting is missing, check the lead actually booked via the link in the outreach email.
